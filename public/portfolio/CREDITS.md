## Audio

No external audio asset is included. The shared sound manager currently produces only brief, low-volume synthesized interface tones after the visitor explicitly enables sound. It remembers the mute preference and suspends audio while the page is hidden.

Room ambience remains intentionally absent because a reliable licensed source was not available during this task. Any future ambience must document its asset name, creator, source URL, license, and modifications here and must not rely on audible autoplay.

## Generated and code-native artwork

- The room environment, open book, and computer prop are original code-native CSS compositions created for this portfolio. They do not copy third-party artwork.
- An attempt to generate a bespoke panoramic room illustration with the built-in image generator was blocked when application network permission was revoked. No generated image was added to the repository.
