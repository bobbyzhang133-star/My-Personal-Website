§ 005 — About

I'm 16, and I'm just getting started

My work lives in the space between game and film — interactive art that, ideally, holds up a mirror to society.

I started making things seriously in the summer of 2025 — a survival horror about killer chickens, a documentary in Inner Mongolia, a science short on flight. Different mediums, same instinct: build something a stranger might feel.

The other half of the practice is code. I run a small lab on HuggingFace where I'm trying to get free language models to generate playable HTML5 games end-to-end — stitching Groq, Qwen, and Pollinations together so no one piece has to be perfect.

Right now I'm a 10th grader at Oaks Christian School in Westlake Village, CA. I'm learning Unreal Engine 5 and C++ in my Software Engineering Game Development course, and I'm collaborating with the Aviation Club on a documentary about rare WWII-era fighters and tanks.

I'm most interested in projects that combine teaching with making — I led friends through GameMaker for the Brackeys Jam, and taught editing on the road in Hulunbuir. Building a thing alone is fine. Building it with someone who's never built before is better.
